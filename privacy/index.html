Privacy Policy for No Ad Apps

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy applies to all apps published by No Ad Apps, including No Ad Hangman and No Ad Currency Converter. Exceptions are noted below for No Ad Meal Planning and No Ad Collections, which use cloud sync to power Household Sharing.

Information We Collect

No Ad Apps does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or user data. We do not know who you are, where you are, or how you use our apps. (See the Cloud-Synced Data sections below for the exceptions, No Ad Meal Planning and No Ad Collections.)

Data Storage

All app settings and preferences are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. (See the Cloud-Synced Data sections below for the exceptions, No Ad Meal Planning and No Ad Collections.)

Cloud-Synced Data (No Ad Meal Planning)

No Ad Meal Planning has a cloud backend, used specifically to power its Household Sharing feature (letting everyone in a family see and edit the same recipes and meal plan in real time). For this app only, the statements above about fully local storage do not apply. Specifically:

  • Recipes, meal plans, ratings, family member profiles (including allergy and dislike tags), and household membership are stored on Google Cloud infrastructure (Firebase/Firestore and Firebase Storage for recipe photos), not only on your device.
  • This data is scoped to your household (identified by a join code, not a personal account) and is only accessible to devices that have joined that household.
  • We still do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or any third-party tracking. Your data is not sold or shared with third parties. It exists solely to make Household Sharing work.
  • Leaving a household or deleting a recipe removes it from shared storage per the choices described in the app itself.
  • Importing a recipe from a link (or sharing one into the app) opens the recipe website in an in-app browser. While you're on a site, you see it as it is, including that site's own ads and cookies, which are governed by that site's own privacy policy.
  • The app clears the in-app browser's cookies, cache, and site data when you close it. Some sites, such as Pinterest, require you to sign in before you can see a recipe. So you don't have to sign in again every time you add a recipe, your sign-in for those sites is kept. It is stored only on your device, and No Ad Apps never collects or tracks it in any way. Signing out of the site inside the in-app browser removes it.
  • We don't collect, see, or track your browsing in the in-app browser. Only the recipe you choose to import is saved, and it's stored like any other recipe (see above). If an import fails, the page's address and the error are recorded, with nothing that identifies you, so we can improve recipe importing.

Contact flinnanenterprises@gmail.com for questions or data deletion requests specific to this app.

Cloud-Synced Data (No Ad Collections)

No Ad Collections also uses a cloud backend, for the same reason: Household Sharing, so everyone in a household sees and edits the same collections in real time. For this app, the statements above about fully local storage do not apply. Specifically:

  • Collections, items, custom fields and the values you enter in them (such as prices or notes), removal reasons, and household membership are stored on Google Cloud infrastructure (Firebase/Firestore), not only on your device.
  • Photos you add to a collection or item, including receipt photos, are uploaded to Firebase Storage. The app keeps its own copy, so deleting a photo from your phone's photo library does not remove it from the app.
  • The app signs in with an anonymous ID. There is no name, email address, or password. Your data is scoped to your household (identified by a join code, not a personal account) and is only accessible to devices that have joined that household.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or any third-party tracking. Your data is not sold or shared with third parties. It exists solely to make the app and Household Sharing work.
  • Permanently deleting an item or collection removes it, and its photos, from shared storage. Leaving a household works as described in the app itself.

Contact flinnanenterprises@gmail.com for questions or data deletion requests specific to this app.

Exchange Rate Data (Currency Converter)

The Currency Converter app fetches publicly available exchange rate data to display current rates. No personal information is sent as part of this request.

Third Parties

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services of any kind.

In-App Purchases

Where applicable, payments are handled entirely by Apple's App Store, and we never see your payment details. Apps with paid upgrades use RevenueCat to check whether an upgrade has been purchased. For apps with Household Sharing, that purchase status is tied to the anonymous household ID so one upgrade covers the whole household.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
